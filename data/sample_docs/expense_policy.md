# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting Expenses

Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense. Reports are
submitted through the finance portal and require manager approval.

## Meals and Travel

During business travel, meals are reimbursed up to 50 dollars per day. Receipts are required
for all individual claims over 25 dollars. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at 0.65 dollars per mile for approved business trips.
Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Personal entertainment, traffic fines, and hotel minibar charges are not reimbursable under
any circumstances.
