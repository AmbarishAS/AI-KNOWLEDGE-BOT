# Vacation and Leave Policy

## Paid Vacation

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation accrues
monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees accrue vacation on a pro-rata
basis according to their contracted hours.

## Rollover

Unused vacation rolls over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year. Any
balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31 unless an exception is approved by HR.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not roll over and
cannot be cashed out upon termination.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit time-off requests through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for planned
vacation. Manager approval is required before the leave is confirmed.
